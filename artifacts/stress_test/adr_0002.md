## Context
L1 System Context
## Decision
Centralize product data in a single source of truth service rather than duplicated across Catalog, Search, and Order services.
## Consequences
This change simplifies data management and ensures consistency across services. However, it may introduce a single point of failure and increase complexity in the system. The team will need to implement robust failover mechanisms and ensure that the new service is properly integrated with existing systems.
