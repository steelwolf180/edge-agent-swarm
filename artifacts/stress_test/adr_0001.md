## Context
L1 System Context
## Decision
Centralize product data in a single source of truth service to improve consistency, simplify maintenance, and reduce operational overhead.
## Consequences
Centralizing product data will streamline updates and reduce duplication. However, it may introduce new risks such as single point of failure and increased complexity in scaling. The current architecture relies on multiple services for data synchronization, which could lead to inconsistencies if not managed properly.
